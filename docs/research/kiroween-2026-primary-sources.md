# Kiroween 2026 rules and 2025 winner precedents

> Historical research note from November 19, 2026. Its early Dead Air category, stack, and hosting proposal predate the current implementation. Use the root README and PRODUCT.md for the implemented product. Treat the ideas and cost checks below as background research, not current build instructions.

Sources accessed November 19, 2026. Amazon Web Services sponsors the event and Devpost administers it. The Official Rules page records a November 16 eligibility update adding Australian residents. [2026 Official Rules, §§1–3](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

## 2026 schedule and eligibility

- Entries opened October 30, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific. Registration and submissions both close **Friday, December 4, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. PST / 5:00 p.m. EST (Toronto)**. The rules exclude late entries. Judging runs from December 16 at 1:00 p.m. Pacific through January 29, 2027 at 1:00 p.m. Pacific. Winners are expected around February 3, 2027 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific. [Official Rules, §1](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- Individuals must have reached their local age of majority. Teams can have up to three eligible members, and eligible organizations can enter. Teams and organizations designate an eligible representative. Geographic and conflict-of-interest exclusions apply. [Official Rules, §3](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

## 2026 submission obligations

- **Project and category.** Build an original working application during the submission window using Kiro IDE, CLI, Web, or Crew. The app must work consistently on its intended platform and deliver what the entry depicts. Standard libraries, frameworks, and starter templates are permitted. Disclose other reused work and have permission to use third-party data, SDKs, APIs, and materials. Select one category. The organizers may disqualify projects for insufficient Kiro use. [Official Rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
  - **Resurrection** revives obsolete technology for a present or future need.
  - **Frankenstein** combines seemingly incompatible technologies into one application with a capability made possible by the combination.
  - **Skeleton Crew** demonstrates a lean, reusable code foundation with two distinct applications. The GitHub repository needs a separate folder for each application.
  - **Costume Contest** uses a polished, haunting interface whose spooky elements improve the experience or functionality.
- **Required Devpost materials.** Submit a description of the features and functionality, the working app or test-build URL, a public YouTube demo link, the public GitHub repository URL, a write-up explaining how Kiro was used, and the category and any bonus selections. Complete every required field and submit the entry before the deadline. Provide English materials or translations. [Official Rules, §§4–5](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Tester access.** Keep the app freely available to judges, the sponsor, and the administrator through January 29 at 1:00 p.m. Pacific. Put any required credentials and testing instructions in Devpost’s designated private field. Judges may base their decision on the written and visual submission materials alone. [Official Rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Repository and video.** Include all source, assets and instructions needed to run the app, a detectable open-source license, and a tracked `.kiro/` directory at the repository root. The demo should run under three minutes and show the app running on its intended device. Judges can stop watching at three minutes. Clear third-party media and trademark rights for the video. [Official Rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Kiro evidence.** The rules require a write-up on effective use and give examples including specs, steering, hooks, MCP, custom agents, property-based testing, Kiro Powers, and the different Kiro surfaces. The judges determine how much Kiro use is sufficient. [Official Rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Optional credit grant.** Eligible participants can request 4,000 Kiro credits by December 1 at noon Pacific. Devpost asks for identity verification and the associated Kiro user ID or AWS account ID. Redeem by December 4. [Official Rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules), [Devpost Resources](https://kiroween.devpost.com/resources)

## 2026 judging and bonus prizes

Stage One is a pass/fail check for theme fit and use of the required tools. Stage Two scores five criteria equally at **20% each and up to 10 points each**. Overall and category prizes use these same scores. An entry can receive one overall or category award and any applicable bonuses. [Official Rules, §§6 and 8](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

| Criterion | What the judges assess |
| --- | --- |
| Potential Impact | Credible problem and audience, usefulness of the demonstrated solution, accessibility, and likelihood of actual use |
| Technological Implementation | How deeply and strategically the team used Kiro, including meaningful use of several capabilities and experimentation |
| Design | Coherent, finished product experience with deliberate, intuitive design |
| Innovation/Idea | Originality and distinctive use or combination of technologies and resources |
| Presentation | Clear and memorable pitch with an end-to-end demo that establishes the problem, audience, and value |

Best Demo needs no additional entry material and goes to the highest Presentation scorer among eligible entries in the overall top 10%. Startup requires founder confirmation, a LinkedIn profile, and a startup site. Student requires every team member to qualify and supply academic details. Ghost of Kiroween Past requires verified 2025 registration and a new 2026 entry. The Crew bonus requires a separate public GitHub repo under the matching account for a new Kiro Crew app, plus a feature write-up. Blog and Social Blitz require public posts during the entry window and links on the form. The blog must name Kiroween 2026 and cover the project and Kiro. The social post must begin with `#KiroweenHackathon26` and explain how a favorite Kiro feature changed the entrant’s development approach. [Official Rules, §§4, 6 and 8](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules), [Devpost event overview](https://kiroween.devpost.com/)

## 2025 winner precedents

The Devpost gallery confirms winner status, and each submission’s award badge identifies the specific prize. The creators report the implementation and Kiro details below. The principles are inferences from those accounts. [2025 winners gallery](https://kiroween-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery)

| Project and award | What the team submitted | Principle supported by the submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **InteractionKit**, overall first place | A Python and MediaPipe foundation for gesture-controlled apps, used to build two distinct products. Math-O-Lantern is a pinch-controlled math game. Holo-Board is a gesture drawing and recording tool. The team used hooks to wire in new gestures, separate specs for each app, and Context7 MCP to ground library references. [Devpost submission](https://devpost.com/software/kiro-interactionkit), [Source repo](https://github.com/Harpita-P/Kiro-InteractionKit) | Two finished examples with different user goals make the value of a shared foundation easy to see. The Kiro hooks were also reusable by people who adopt the kit. |
| **SpookBnB**, overall third place, entered in Costume Contest | A vacation rental site whose dark-mode switch reveals a flashlight-guided escape room. Reviews, page content, and reservation UI become clues in a four-part puzzle. The team kept the specs current as mechanics changed and used steering and file-save hooks. [Devpost submission](https://devpost.com/software/spookbnb-when-dark-mode-gets-truly-dark) | A familiar interaction communicates the premise immediately. The theme drives the actions a player takes and the way the game progresses. |
| **Faxi**, Best Resurrection and Influencer Judges’ Choice | A fax-to-digital prototype aimed at seniors in Japan. It turns handwritten fax requests into actions such as two-way email and faxes results back. The creator used Kiro specs, hooks, steering, and custom MCP tools. A requirement for confirming an action by circling an option on a fax shaped the interface and backend. [Devpost submission](https://devpost.com/software/faxi-16iecy) | A legacy channel can be valuable because the audience already knows how to use it. A concrete requirement tied to that audience can guide otherwise complex integrations. |
| **RumorNet**, Best Frankenstein | A multilingual dashboard combining network-level signals for coordination and echo chambers with analysis of individual posts. The creator reports using Kiro specs for the dashboard, iterative prompting for the analysis agents, and filesystem and Git MCP for restructuring the code for AWS Lambda. [Devpost submission](https://devpost.com/software/rumornet) | A combination is easier to explain when its parts answer complementary questions. The write-up identifies concrete Kiro workflow choices, failed attempts, and changes prompted by deployment constraints. |

Project-level judge scores and reasons for the awards remain unverified. Reported capabilities such as RumorNet’s language coverage and Faxi’s usability for seniors are creator claims. [2025 winners gallery](https://kiroween-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery), [RumorNet submission](https://devpost.com/software/rumornet), [Faxi submission](https://devpost.com/software/faxi-16iecy)

## Zero-cost feasibility and originality scan

Sources below were checked November 19, 2026. Free tiers and limits can change; verify again before deployment.

- **Kiro development cost.** Kiro Free is $0 and currently includes 50 credits per month with free-tier models subject to rate limits; the tier does not offer add-on credits. A user must stop rather than upgrade or buy credits to satisfy a hard $0 cap. Separately, the Kiroween rules say eligible entrants may request a one-time 4,000-credit participant grant; approval is not guaranteed, and the request deadline is Dec. 1 at noon Pacific. The product and its demo should not depend on this grant. [Kiro pricing](https://kiro.dev/pricing/), [Kiroween rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Static hosting.** Cloudflare Pages can host a static-only project for $0: Cloudflare says free accounts need no credit card, static asset requests are free and unlimited, and Pages Free has 500 builds per month, 20,000 files per site, and a 25 MiB per-file limit. Use no Pages Functions, paid storage, custom domain, or external account-bound runtime. Judges can open the public app URL without paying or logging in. [Cloudflare Free](https://www.cloudflare.com/), [Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/), [static asset pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/functions/pricing/)
- **Application runtime.** Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript plus browser-local storage, DOM parsing, file downloads, and print rendering require no API, model, database, runtime package CDN, or recurring service charge. Each proposal below uses fictional bundled sample content or user input, and original CSS/SVG assets. Any library used during development should be bundled locally with its license included.
- **Repository.** A public GitHub repository and open-source license are required by the competition. That alone does not guarantee ongoing availability, so the suggested public static URL should be kept live through the judging period. [Kiroween rules, §4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)
- **Adjacent products found.** Offline household planning and outage simulation already exist, including [Outage Ready](https://github.com/kennethkimbrough/outage-ready), [We Should Go](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.limestonelabs.weshouldgo), and [Bereitsein](https://bereitsein.app/en/vorstellung/). A community communication-network exercise would need to stay focused on volunteer-run hubs and stress-testing information paths, rather than reproducing household inventory or emergency guidance. Preparedness tabletop products also exist; [Action Stations](https://actionstations.ai/) generates exercises from an organization’s own documents. The proposed project should keep its value in a fast, low-data visualization of who loses information when channels fail.
- **Adjacent archival tools found.** Free/open-source museum tools, self-contained storybooks, and online exhibition builders already exist, including [Exhibitera](https://exhibitera.org/), [CollectionBuilder](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/), and [Lumino Storybook](https://luminostudio.app/). An artifact archive candidate would need a narrower audience and distinctive workflow to be compelling.
- **QR library for the resurrection candidate.** The `qrcode` package’s upstream repository identifies an MIT license and a browser bundle. The candidate can bundle that source and license notice locally, with no QR service or network calls at runtime. [node-qrcode repository](https://github.com/soldair/node-qrcode), [MIT license](https://github.com/soldair/node-qrcode/blob/master/license)

## Candidate ideas and judgment estimates

These are product hypotheses from the current brief and quick competitor scan, not evidence of judge outcomes. Scores are 1–10 for comparison only; each 2026 rubric criterion is equally weighted. “Feasibility” accounts for the user's advanced experience, solo build, under-20-hour budget, and Dec. 4 deadline.

| Working concept | Category | Impact | Kiro/technical | Design | Innovation | Demo | Feasibility | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Dead Air** — a local-first facilitator tool that shows which community-hub roles become isolated when a communications channel fails, then prints a simple alternate relay plan | Frankenstein | 8 | 8 | 9 | 8 | 9 | 9 | Keep it a group communications exercise, not household emergency software or safety advice; adjacent tabletop tools exist. |
| **Cabinet After Hours** — capture a few community objects on a phone and publish them as one portable, offline mini-exhibit | Costume Contest | 6 | 7 | 9 | 7 | 9 | 8 | Museum/exhibit and storybook tools overlap; differentiate around a tightly guided temporary pop-up exhibit. |
| **Dead Letter** — revive the pager as a short text payload that can be passed face-to-face by displaying a QR code | Resurrection | 5 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 9 | 8 | One-way, proximity-only transfer is much narrower than internet-free messaging; two-device demo required. The MIT QR generator can be bundled. |
| **Specter Check** — an offline, local-file accessibility review that makes contrast and keyboard-navigation problems visible as a guided haunted inspection | Costume Contest | 8 | 8 | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 | Existing accessibility checkers set a high bar; keep the scope narrow and avoid claiming a full audit. |
| **Skeleton Signal Kit** — a reusable offline field-card template demonstrated by two small apps, a community relay map and an artifact story card | Skeleton Crew | 6 | 9 | 7 | 7 | 8 | 5 | Two distinct applications are required, making it the riskiest fit for under 20 hours. |

**Recommended direction: Dead Air.** Its narrow audience and core interaction make a stronger pitch than a general preparedness app: a volunteer facilitator watches a fictional community center's information network break, repairs the relay path, and exports a one-page follow-up plan. The graph and scenarios remain local; no names, sensitive household inventory, or user accounts are needed. A haunted “signal map” makes failure visible, giving the design a functional role. The main feasibility condition is to keep the scenario set to one fictional community hub and a few channels.

**Proposed zero-cost stack:** Kiro IDE Free for planned specs and implementation within its free credit allowance; static HTML/CSS/JavaScript; local browser storage; no runtime AI/API or paid data; original visual assets; public GitHub repo; Cloudflare Pages Free for the functional URL. Static asset hosting can remain free through judging within published limits, subject to Cloudflare account and service availability. The output repo still needs its open-source license and root `.kiro/` directory. [Kiro pricing](https://kiro.dev/pricing/), [Cloudflare Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/), [Kiroween rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

**Three risks:** novelty against existing exercise tools; scenario design drifting into inaccurate emergency advice; and Kiro's 50-credit monthly free allowance being too small for an unfocused build. Mitigate these by targeting community-hub communication routing, including no procedural safety advice, and using Kiro on a small spec, an architecture decision, and a local validation hook before implementing the narrow MVP.
